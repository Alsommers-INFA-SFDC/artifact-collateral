Environment Overview

Environment Name

b2b2c — Informatica MDM SaaS demonstration environment for B2B2C distribution network master data management. Models the full channel from supplier through distributor and carrier to end customer, including product registration, household relationships, employment, and carrier-provided product agreements. Includes an Apple US B2B device distribution scenario as the reference use case.

Accelerator vs. Custom Model

8 of 11 entities are shipped Informatica 360 accelerator content — the highest accelerator-to-custom ratio of all six Business 360 environments. Two accelerator entities are renamed:
  • c360.person → Customers (B2C individual consumer)
  • c360.organization → Accounts (B2B enterprise account)

Smallest and Most Focused Environment

With 11 entities, 14 relationships, and 6 hierarchies, b2b2c is the most concise of the six Business 360 environments. Its design prioritises depth over breadth — the 14 relationships are densely connected across a small entity graph, making it well-suited for demonstrating Informatica MDM's relationship and hierarchy management capabilities without the complexity of the larger product-master environments.

Apple US B2B Reference Scenario

The Apple US B2B Categorization hierarchy and the Carrier Agreement / Carrier Provides Product relationships are modelled on an Apple US B2B device distribution scenario — supplier (Apple) → distributor/carrier → enterprise account → individual employee. This makes the environment particularly relevant for technology distribution, telecom, and device-as-a-service demonstrations.

Dual-Identity Customer Model

Unlike most environments that model either B2B or B2C customers, b2b2c explicitly models both: Customers (c360.person) for consumers and household members, and Accounts (c360.organization) for corporate buyers — linked by the Employment relationship. This dual-identity graph is the defining architectural feature of the environment.
