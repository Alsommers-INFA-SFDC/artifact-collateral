Environment Overview

Environment Name

GDSNGrocery — Informatica MDM SaaS demonstration environment for grocery product master data management, built on the Global Data Synchronisation Network (GDSN) standard. Combines a full product-to-supply-chain model with a quick-service restaurant (QSR) menu-mastering overlay.

Designed for retailer and CPG customer-facing demonstrations, data model workshops, and proof-of-concept engagements.

Use Cases & Capabilities

GDSN Trade Item Synchronisation

  • Onboard and validate trade items against GS1 standards
  • Manage GTINs, barcodes, and packaging hierarchies
  • Synchronise item attributes with trading partners
  • Track item lifecycle from creation to discontinuation

Product Content & Enrichment

  • Manage marketing copy, images, and digital assets
  • Enrich items with nutritional, allergen, and regulatory data
  • Maintain variant and product-line relationships
  • Drive e-commerce product syndication

Supplier & Vendor Management

  • Golden record for vendor/supplier organisations
  • Link items to manufacturing, packaging, and warehouse locations
  • Manage supplier hierarchy and contact relationships
  • Track supplier performance and certifications

Supply Chain & Packaging

  • Model full GDSN packaging hierarchy (unit → inner → case → pallet)
  • Track production, packaging, and warehousing locations
  • Support material-level manufacturing and BOM traceability
  • Contract manufacturing and cross-dock scenarios

QSR Menu Mastering

  • Master menu catalogs with item, recipe, and ingredient hierarchies
  • Manage modifier groups, customisations, and substitutions
  • Track ingredient availability by location
  • Support menu evolution and seasonal changes

Product Classification & Taxonomy

  • GS1 / GDSN category taxonomy management
  • SAP material classification alignment
  • Attribute management and schema governance
  • Multi-taxonomy cross-mapping

Key Integration Points

Upstream Sources

  • GDSN Data Pool — trade item onboarding via GS1 data synchronisation
  • SAP / ERP — material groups, cost centres, purchasing data
  • Supplier Portals — vendor onboarding, product specs, compliance docs
  • PIM Systems — marketing copy, digital assets, channel-specific content
  • QSR Operations Platform — menu lifecycle, location availability, modifier configs

Downstream Consumers

  • E-commerce / DTC — enriched product content syndication
  • Retailer Trading Partners — GDSN-compliant item synchronisation
  • Supply Chain Systems — location-linked item master for logistics
  • POS / In-Store — item and category data for register systems
  • Menu Boards & Ordering Apps — real-time menu and modifier data

Accelerator vs. Custom Model

12 of 22 entities are shipped Informatica 360 accelerator content — they carry forward standard data models, match rules, and relationship definitions out of the box. 10 entities are custom-built for the grocery/QSR demo context. Two accelerator entities have been renamed in this environment:
  • s360.supplier → Vendor (reflects retail industry terminology)
  • infa360rtl_product → Product Collection (used for assortment grouping)

Dual-Domain Design

This environment is intentionally dual-purpose: the core GDSN grocery model handles trade-item and supply-chain mastering, while the QSR menu-mastering overlay (Menu Catalog → Recipe → Ingredient → Modifier Group) demonstrates Informatica MDM's flexibility for food-service and restaurant data. Both domains share the same Location, Supplier, and Material entities, avoiding duplication.
