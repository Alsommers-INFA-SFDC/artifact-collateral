Use Cases & Capabilities

Material & Product Master

  • Golden record for raw materials, semi-finished goods, and finished products
  • Material category and classification taxonomy management
  • Align material master to SAP and ERP systems of record
  • Track material specs, certifications, and regulatory attributes

Bill of Materials (BOM)

  • Multi-level BOM from raw material → component → semi-finished → finished good
  • BOM traceability for quality, recall, and compliance
  • Gas product ingredient-to-solution BOM modeling
  • Rentable vs. sellable material differentiation

Manufacturing Operations

  • Link materials and products to production plants and locations
  • Track contract manufacturing relationships
  • Semi-finished good build-at location management
  • Ship-to location mapping for finished goods

Legal Entity & Corporate Structure

  • Hierarchical legal entity master (parent company → subsidiaries → business units)
  • Operating region assignment and regional hierarchy
  • Business unit to product ownership mapping
  • Cross-entity ownership and supporting entity relationships

Customer & Distribution

  • Customer and household relationship modeling
  • Distributor-to-product assignment
  • Account equipment tracking (cylinder / tank management for gas)
  • Guarantor and service-by relationships

Supplier Management

  • Supplier golden record with contact hierarchy
  • Raw material sourced-from supplier traceability
  • Supplier hierarchy and parent-subsidiary structures
  • Special paper / contract order management

Key Integration Points

Upstream Sources

  • SAP / ERP — material master, BOM, plant data, cost centres
  • PLM System — product specifications and engineering BOMs
  • Supplier Portals — vendor onboarding, raw material certs
  • Contract Management — contract manufacturing agreements
  • CRM — customer account and distributor data

Downstream Consumers

  • ERP / SAP — cleansed material and BOM master synchronisation
  • Supply Chain Planning — sourcing, production scheduling
  • Quality Management — BOM traceability for recall and audit
  • Customer & Sales Systems — product-to-customer distribution data
  • Regulatory Reporting — legal entity and compliance structures

Accelerator vs. Custom Model

10 of 15 entities are shipped Informatica 360 accelerator content. 5 entities — Legal Entity, Business Unit, Region, Customer, Household — are custom-built for this manufacturing and industrial-sector demo context. One accelerator entity has been renamed:
  • c360.person → Farmer Customer (scoped to agricultural customer segments)

Specialty Gas Industry Overlay

Several relationships and hierarchies are purpose-built for a specialty industrial gas scenario: Solution Uses Gas, Rentable Tank to Sellable Material, Account Equipment, and the Gas Products / Tech Solutions hierarchies. These demonstrate Informatica MDM's flexibility for asset-heavy, contract-driven industries where materials, products, and customer equipment are tightly linked.

Legal Entity Depth

Uniquely among the Business 360 environments, manufacture includes a full Legal Entity hierarchy alongside the standard Customer 360 organization entity — supporting corporate structure governance, ownership traceability, and compliance use cases beyond the typical product or customer master.
